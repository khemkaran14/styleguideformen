## 1. Master prompt (send once, with the reference photo attached)

```
I am building a men’s style guide website. I will ask you for a photo package of ONE male model: the man in the attached photo.

Rules for EVERY image:
- SAME MODEL IN EVERY IMAGE: the man in the attached reference photo. Keep his face, skin tone, eyes, eyebrows, nose and build exactly the same in every image. Photorealistic fashion catalogue photography, not illustration.
- Keep his own hair (medium-length, dark, wavy, swept back) and his short full beard, unless I say otherwise for that image.
- Plain seamless studio background in light warm grey (#ECEAE6), soft even studio lighting, soft shadow under the feet, no props, no text, no logos, no watermark.
- Sharp focus, true-to-life fabric colours and textures, natural Indian skin tone.

There are three kinds of image:
A) OUTFIT (full body): portrait 4:5 (1080×1350). Head to toe including shoes, standing straight, facing the camera, arms relaxed at the sides, body centred with a little space above the head and below the feet. Neutral confident expression.
B) HAIRSTYLE (head and shoulders): square 1:1 (1080×1080). Facing the camera, shoulders visible, plain charcoal crew-neck tee. Change ONLY his hair to the style I describe; keep his face and his short beard.
C) BEARD (head and shoulders): square 1:1 (1080×1080). Same framing and tee as B. Keep his own hair; change ONLY the facial hair to the style I describe.

Name every file exactly as I give it (e.g. looks/navysuit.jpg) and when I say “zip”, put the images into a zip keeping the folder names looks/, hair/ and beards/.
Reply “Ready” and wait for my first batch.
```
